## Verified cause and correction

The #137 review raised an unverified cast observation. Full related #126/#172/#174/#176/#191/#213/#351 histories
were refreshed/read; the all-state inventory and retained comments were checked by underlying mechanism. A distinct
issue was filed only after the original-production reproduction. The cast predates #137, whose historical report
is unchanged. Closed #174's refuted lock-deadlock allegation is not reopened.

After retaining an offer as ACCEPTING and cancelling its offer timer, the dispatcher invokes destination preflight
and `openSink()`. Its old catch cast `failureFromCause(...)` to `FileTransferFailed`, although that helper deliberately
preserves `AuthenticationFailed`. A custom destination throwing that type therefore caused `ClassCastException`
before active-entry retirement, abort, Failed publication or terminal response. Tests observed Offered, one pending
offer, zero aborts and zero results even beyond the original offer deadline.

Both local setup catches now use a statically `FileTransferFailed`-typed helper. It preserves existing file-error
identity and otherwise wraps the local cause using STORAGE / ACCEPT / RETRY_AFTER_USER_ACTION. The general classifier
still preserves genuine **channel** authentication failures. No unchecked cast, plaintext fallback or new wire result
was added. The correction reaches established retirement, bounded abort and sanitized terminal/replay handling.
