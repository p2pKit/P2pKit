normal/error/transport-cancellation completion. Active recovery/backoff, structural cancellation and late raw/setup
ownership remain intact. Already-admitted work is not synchronously cancelled at the sealing instruction.

parent cancellation. The real-kit clean-stop test now rejects every unexpected warning/error rather than one
substring. #133's mixed-profile warning net was not relaxed.
