Mac27/Xcode27 environment and the existing native executor without changing its
ownership, cancellation, wrapper-stop or finalization requirements.

## Exact scope

`scripts/rpc_cli_process_controls.py` admits only a newly source-built
`:p2p-sample-desktop:installDist` runtime. Each JAR and the three focused JVM
test reports are bound to the frozen source. It runs **85 selected cases**:

- 29 launch-option cases: help, unknown options, duplicate/blank values,
  malformed reconnect, invalid diagnostics options and oversized input;
- the REPL command/diagnostics matrix, including bounded oversized-line
  rejection, complete-line draining, continued usability, export and clear;
- a three-peer manual-loopback matrix: ambiguous aliases, pinned connection,
  actual broadcast/targeted receipt, close and an unavailable peer;
- three source-admission pressure cases: two unfinished loopback connections,
  refusal of the third, then authenticated recovery;
- three 49 MiB transfer cases: consent rejection, receiver-private directory
  permission denial, restoration and exact successful file hash;
- 48 interruption cases: TERM, KILL, EOF, STOP/CONT and HUP during handshake,
  idle and transfer, plus sender KILL while receiving, each repeated three times.
  STOP lasts 35 seconds, beyond the unchanged 30-second keepalive limit.

The handshake/transfer phase gate forwards only unchanged opaque loopback bytes.
It is not a real-network impairment or cryptographic-interoperability claim.
Every peer has a separate private profile; same/new-profile restarts must reset
the sample's explicitly process-local identity. No production security toggle,
SDK hook, firewall/interface change or test timeout extension is permitted.

## Safety and evidence

Launch at most three synthetic peers concurrently, each with a 256 MiB maximum
Java heap. Observe owned-lifetime RSS, CPU counters, threads and descriptors at
phase boundaries and approximately five-second intervals; stop on a 1 GiB RSS,
512-thread, 2,048-descriptor, 8 MiB terminal-log or 2,700-second campaign cutoff.
Resource-pressure peers additionally lower **only their own** soft descriptor
and process limits to at most 256 and 512. This is not descriptor/process-quota
exhaustion. Keep at least 6 GiB disk headroom at admission; never fill the disk or
purge owner data. Fixture and transfer byte budgets are explicit in the source.

Fault delivery requires a live direct child, matching UID/real UID and kernel
lifetime/parent identity, and its captured opaque audit token. An exec change or
denied signal fails closed: no raw PID kill, name scan, token fabrication or
fallback authority. The enclosing original native executor owns final cleanup.

Retain per-case results, native receipts, child limits, source/runtime manifest,
stdin/fault timeline, terminal logs, resources, immutable pre-fault ZIP snapshots,
final diagnostic exports and receiver filesystem/hash observations. Verify ZIP
member bounds/checksums and source/session membership without extraction. The
existing exporter samples summary and events separately; do not claim an atomic
event-count snapshot. Intentional KILL is never labeled graceful cleanup. Empty
destination reservations and staging left by a crash are not successful files;
preserve them for reconciliation. Catchable exits must retire staging/reservations.

verification → focused CLI tests/producer → CLI process controls/finalization.

phone or mobile-driver suites. No automatic retry, consumed bootstrap reuse or
artifact relabeling is allowed. A source fix requires a new frozen candidate and
fresh admission; previous failures remain preserved. The changed offline policy
suite is `python3 -B scripts/tests/rpc-cli-process-controls-test.py`.
